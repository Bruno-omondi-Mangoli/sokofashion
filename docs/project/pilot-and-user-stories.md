# Pilot and user stories

## Pilot shop profile (from the owner's five friends)

- Products: women's clothes, shoes and handbags.
- Staff: some shops have one attendant, at least one owner works alone. Exact numbers to be confirmed.
- Customer payments: cash and M-Pesa. M-Pesa is received through Paybill, Buy Goods Till or Pochi la Biashara.
- Selling channels today: WhatsApp Status, Facebook and Instagram.
- Storefront need: show each product from several angles.

## Seller payment methods at launch

- M-Pesa Buy Goods Till
- M-Pesa Paybill
- M-Pesa Pochi la Biashara (number based)
- Cash

For all M-Pesa methods the buyer pays the seller and submits the M-Pesa code, the seller confirms it, and a code can never be used on two orders. Cash is recorded by the seller or attendant.

## User stories: buyer

- As a buyer, I want to see each item from several angles, so that I can judge it before paying.
- As a buyer, I want to pick my size and colour and see if it is in stock, so that I do not order something unavailable.
- As a buyer, I want to order without creating an account, so that checkout is quick.
- As a buyer, I want clear instructions on how to pay the seller by M-Pesa, so that I am sure I paid the right place.
- As a buyer, I want a digital receipt, so that I have proof of my order.
- As a buyer, I want the page to load fast on a cheap phone, so that I do not waste data.

## User stories: seller (owner)

- As a seller, I want a link to my shop that I can share on WhatsApp Status in one tap, so that customers can browse and order without chatting back and forth.
- As a seller, I want to add a product with several photos, sizes and colours from my phone, so that I can list stock quickly.
- As a seller, I want to see all orders in one place, so that none are lost in chats.
- As a seller, I want to confirm an M-Pesa code against an order, so that I know who has paid.
- As a seller, I want the system to refuse a code that was already used, so that nobody pays once and claims two orders.
- As a seller, I want to record cash sales, so that my stock and money records stay complete.
- As a seller, I want to see what I sold, spent and earned each day, so that I know my real profit.
- As a seller, I want to record customers who owe me, so that I can follow up.
- As a seller, I want to add my attendant with limited permissions, so that she can sell but cannot change records.

## User stories: attendant

- As an attendant, I want to record a sale quickly, so that customers are not kept waiting.
- As an attendant, I want to see what is in stock, so that I do not promise what we do not have.
- As an attendant, I want to request a refund or void instead of doing it myself, so that I am protected from blame.

## User stories: platform admin (the owner of this project)

- As the admin, I want to create, suspend and reactivate shops, so that I control who is on the platform.
- As the admin, I want to set a shop's plan by hand, so that I can run the pilot without automated billing.

## Pilot success numbers (hypotheses, adjust after interviews)

Measured over 4 weeks with 5 shops.

- 5 of 5 shops publish a storefront and share it on WhatsApp Status in the first week.
- Each shop lists at least 15 products with at least 3 photos each.
- At least 3 of 5 shops receive 5 or more orders through the storefront.
- At least 90 percent of online orders have an M-Pesa code recorded and confirmed within 24 hours.
- The first page of a storefront loads in under 3 seconds on a throttled mobile connection.
- Zero cases of one shop seeing another shop's data.
- At least 4 of 5 owners say in the final interview they want to keep using it.
- At least 3 of 5 owners say they would pay a price we name.

## Questions for each pilot friend

1. How many products do you sell, and how many photos do you take of each?
2. How many staff do you have, and what do they do?
3. How do you take payment today, and how do you check an M-Pesa message is real?
4. Have you ever lost stock or money? How, and how did you find out?
5. How many orders come through WhatsApp on a normal day?
6. Do you deliver? To where, and how do you charge for it?
7. What do you pay for tools today, if anything?
8. What would you be willing to pay per month for this, and what would make it a clear yes?
9. Which phone do you use and how much data do you buy?
