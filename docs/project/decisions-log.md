D050: Stock is an append only ledger. Attendants can read stock but not change it. The request and approval workflow for staff stock adjustments is part of the anti theft module in Phase 9. Reason: stock theft hides behind editable numbers, and scope must stay disciplined for MVP v1. Status: active.
D051: Instead of generating TypeScript types from the database now, every result from the database is validated with Zod before the app uses it. D055: Photos are resized in the browser before upload to a main picture of at most 1200 pixels and a thumbnail of at most 400 pixels, as WebP where the browser can make it and JPEG otherwise, each file under 500 KB. They upload straight from the browser to a public storage bucket. The database works out every file name from the shop, product and photo ids, and requires both files to exist before it registers a photo. Each product has 1 to 4 photos and each shop is capped at 100 MB until plan limits exist. Reason: weak connections and costly data, the 1 GB free storage, and the rule that the browser never chooses a path. Status: active.
D056: A product cannot be live without at least one photo (D048), enforced by a database trigger that applies to everybody including administrators. Reason: buyers must never see a product with no picture, and the rule must not depend on the screens. Status: active.

D059: Photos are never cropped. They are stored in their original shape, only scaled down, and every screen shows the whole photo at its natural shape, or fitted inside its box for small thumbnails. D060: A break it on purpose check must first prove the break took effect, by querying the database state (for example pg_trigger) and by working in the local database through a command that prints a confirmation, not only in a browser editor. D061: Manual stock changes are recorded in everyday words (new stock arrived, customer return, damaged or spoiled, count correction up or down) and turned into ledger entries. Count corrections require a note of at least 3 characters. Undo adds an opposite entry and never erases the original. Attendants see stock levels but not the history or the form. The history says you or a team member until real names come with the audit trail in Phase 9, and dates are shown in Kenyan time until shops get their own time zone. Reason: sellers think in these words, and a correction without a reason is how losses hide. Status: active.
D062: Archiving hides a product from buyers and keeps its stock, photos and history. Restoring brings it back as a draft, and going live again needs at least one photo. Products are never deleted through the app. Reason: orders, receipts and audits will refer to products, and archiving is reversible. Status: active.
